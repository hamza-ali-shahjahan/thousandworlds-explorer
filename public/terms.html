What this is

The explorer lets you browse discovered exoplanets, view simulated climates, and build hypothetical worlds. It is provided free of charge for learning and exploration.

What the predictions are — and are not

The climates, temperatures, and "worlds" shown here are simulated analogies built on the ThousandWorlds climate benchmark and the NASA Exoplanet Archive. They are not observations, not predictions of what a real planet is actually like, and not habitability claims. Treat them as a way to build intuition and ask questions, not as scientific conclusions or measurements.
